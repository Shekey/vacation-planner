# Vacation Planner: project context

A snapshot of how this app was planned and built, so a later session (human or Claude) can pick it up without the original chat. Exported 2026-10-02.

To continue work in a new Claude session, point it at this file: "Read docs/project-context.md first."

## What it is

A multi-tenant web app where people book vacation inside a workspace (a team). Admins invite members; nobody joins without an invite. Live at https://vacation-planner-lyart.vercel.app (Vercel + Neon Postgres).

## Decisions (from Ajdin)

- Workspace = team. No nested sub-teams.
- Admin invites only. Anyone can sign in and create their own workspace.
- Yearly allowance is tracked, with days left shown. Half-day bookings are allowed.
- Carry-over (Ajdin, 2026-10-03): per-workspace max days plus an optional "must be taken by" day (`carryOverExpiry`, "MM-DD"). Carried days are used first; what's left of them after that day expires (`carriedOverStatus` in `src/lib/booking-days.ts`).
- Approval workflow is an optional per-workspace flag. Admin-created bookings skip approval.
- Stack: Next.js (App Router) + TypeScript, Postgres + Prisma, Auth.js magic links via Resend.
- Notifications go to Microsoft Teams (webhook); Slack was added as an option in round 2 (2026-10-03).
- Data minimization (Ajdin, 2026-10-03): no sick leave and no free-text notes are stored, to keep legal exposure low; bookings are deleted 3 years after they end (`src/lib/retention.ts`, run by the monthly holidays cron).
- Sick days (Ajdin, 2026-10-22, #59): back as a private "Sick" type with dates only (no notes, no documents; the employer gets the eAU from the health insurer). Never counts, never needs approval, admins get an email when a member reports sick. Colleagues, chat posts and every iCal feed see "Other" (`forViewer`). Sick days inside a vacation (BUrlG §9): only admins enter them, the vacation is split and the days come back (`giveBackVacation` in `src/lib/bookings.ts`, `spanWithout` in `src/lib/booking-days.ts`). Sick bookings are deleted 12 months after they end. Special leave (Sonderurlaub, #61): own type `SPECIAL`, approval like vacation, never counts; optional reason from a fixed list (`SpecialReason`), shown only to the person and admins. #34 keeps admin-defined types and the stand-in.
- Payments: Stripe (Ajdin chose it over Paddle, 2026-10-03). Flat team prices (raised again 2026-10-18 after a competitor check): Free ≤5, Team €39 ≤25, Business €89 ≤75; yearly is 10× monthly. Assisted team import is €99 once, free with a yearly Business plan (`SETUP_SERVICE_EUR`).
- Target customers (2026-10-17): teams of 5 to 75 without an IT department where someone must always be in: practices, care services, Kitas, trades, restaurants, offices. Plan and roadmap in `docs/sales-plan.md`.
- No CSV export.
- Languages (Ajdin, 2026-10-03): English and German everywhere, picked by the user. First visit follows the browser language, falling back to English. EN/DE switch in the header (landing and app) and on the profile page, where "Browser language" clears the saved choice; saved on the profile when signed in, in a `lang` cookie otherwise. The app UI uses informal "du". Legal pages: German is binding, English is a convenience translation (`?lang=de` / `?lang=en` switches one page).
- Public holidays come per country with a region (ISO 3166-2, e.g. `DE-BE`). Each person picks their own region; the workspace has a default. Focus is Germany: Berlin = `DE-BE`, Bielefeld = North Rhine-Westphalia = `DE-NW`.

## Where things live

- Translations: `src/lib/i18n/` (`config.ts` languages and detection, `messages/<namespace>.ts` one file per area with `en` and `de`, `server.ts` `getLocale()`/`getMessages()` for server code, `index.ts` `messagesFor(locale)`), `useI18n()` in `src/components/i18n-provider.tsx` for client components. A test checks German has every English key. Emails go out in the recipient's saved language, else the workspace language; Teams/Slack posts, the daily digest and holiday-import errors use the workspace language (Settings, `WorkspaceSettings.locale`, set from the creator's language). Holidays keep the local name in `name` and the English one in `englishName` (`holidayName()` in `src/lib/holidays.ts`); older imports get the English name on the next monthly refresh or re-import.
- Schema: `prisma/schema.prisma`, migrations in `prisma/migrations/`.
- Day counting and allowance: `src/lib/booking-days.ts` (`proratedAllowance`, `allowanceIn`, work days in `DayRules`), past years' allowances in `AllowanceYear` (written by `keepLastYearsAllowance` before an admin changes one), `src/lib/bookings.ts` (`regionOf`, `rulesFor`, `forViewer`, `loadHolidays`, `allowanceSummary`).
- Plans and billing: `src/lib/plans.ts` (`accessOf`, limits), `src/lib/stripe.ts`, `/api/stripe/webhook`, `/w/[slug]/billing`. Chat is dropped in `settingsOf` when the plan has none.
- Chat posts: `src/lib/notify.ts` sends to `src/lib/teams.ts` and `src/lib/slack.ts`, keeps the last failure per channel on the workspace (`WorkspaceSettings.teamsError`/`slackError` + `…At`, cleared by the next post that arrives or a new URL) and reports it to Sentry. Settings → *Chat connection* shows it with what to do and has a *Send test* button. Failed emails go to Sentry too (status only, no addresses).
- Legal texts: `src/app/(legal)/`, operator details and subprocessors in `src/lib/legal.ts` (env `LEGAL_*`).
- Account export and deletion: `src/lib/account.ts`, `/api/account/export`, `/account`.
- Holidays: `src/lib/holidays.ts` (Nager parser), `src/lib/holiday-regions.ts` (country and region lists), `src/lib/holiday-import.ts`.
- Tips: `src/lib/smart-days.ts`.
- Team-type presets for new workspaces: `src/lib/team-types.ts`.
- Pages: `src/app/w/[slug]/` (overview, book, calendar, approvals, members, people, holidays, settings, me).
- Crons (`vercel.json`): `/api/cron/daily-digest` daily 06:00 UTC (the Teams/Slack digest skips weekends; trial reminder emails go out every day, `src/lib/trial-reminders.ts`), `/api/cron/holidays` monthly. Both need `CRON_SECRET`.
- Sample team: `src/lib/sample-team.ts` (names per team type, bookings), `src/lib/sample-team-db.ts` (add/remove), buttons in `src/app/w/[slug]/sample-team-button.tsx`. Sample people are `Membership.isSample` with a placeholder user at `<random>@sample.invalid`: never emailed (`sendEmail` drops them), can't sign in (`src/auth.ts`), not counted toward plan limits or the checklist, skipped by chat posts and the digest, labelled "Sample".
- Tests: unit tests next to the code (`*.test.ts`, `npm test`); tests against Postgres (`*.db.test.ts`, `npm run test:db`, helpers in `src/test/`) for the paths where a regression costs people days off or money: who may book, approvals, overlaps, plan limits, allowance and carry-over, cancel/restore, members, invitations, Stripe sync. Each test makes its own workspace; email, chat and the request language are mocked in `src/test/db-setup.ts`. Add one when you change those rules.
- Build: `scripts/vercel-build.mjs` migrates on production, skips migrations on preview.
- Mail to strangers (2026-10-22, #69): invitations and "where to sign in" mails count per workspace per day in `ColdMail` (`src/lib/mail-limits.ts`: 200 a day per workspace, set generously for the beta; lower it for unpaid workspaces when the beta ends); one person can create 3 workspaces a day and 10 in all; workspace names can't hold links or addresses. Sign-in link flooding by address is #72.
- People without email (`src/lib/no-email-members.ts`): adding an address that is new saves it and mails where to sign in. If the address already has an account, nothing moves until that account accepts an invitation (#73): `Invitation.membershipId` points at the placeholder, and `acceptInvitation` moves the membership with its bookings onto the account (or into the membership it got meanwhile). Removing the placeholder revokes the invitation.
- Security: headers in `next.config.ts`; helpers in `src/lib/security.ts`; magic-link throttle (3 per address per 10 min) in `src/lib/sign-in-limit.ts`. The sign-in email links to `/sign-in/confirm` (`src/lib/sign-in-link.ts`), which only uses the one-time token when the person presses the button, so Outlook/Defender link scanners can't use it up; the "check your email" page shows the address and can resend.
- Landing page (2026-10-17, round 2): own look instead of template SaaS: Bricolage Grotesque headings and Caveat handwriting via next/font (self-hosted), looping product demo in the hero (`src/components/landing/hero-demo.tsx`), team-type tabs with scenario calendars (`team-switcher.tsx`), price slider (`price-picker.tsx`), line icons (`icons.tsx`), scroll reveal (`reveal.tsx`). No emoji icons, no gradient blobs.
- App navigation: everyday pages first, admin pages grouped under an "Admin" label; on phones a fixed bottom bar (`src/components/bottom-nav.tsx`) with Home, Calendar, Book, People, Me.
- Look: theme tokens (`background`, `surface`, `muted`, `border`, `primary`) and `.btn`, `.btn-secondary`, `.card`, `.card-link` in `src/app/globals.css`; `Avatar`, `LogoMark`, `EmptyState` in `src/components/ui.tsx`.

## Deploy notes

- Env vars: a Postgres URL (`DATABASE_URL` or Neon's `POSTGRES_*`), `AUTH_SECRET`, `AUTH_RESEND_KEY`, `EMAIL_FROM`, `CRON_SECRET`. See `README.md`.
- Leave `AUTH_URL` unset on Vercel; the app uses the production domain. Never use `vacation-planner.vercel.app`: that is someone else's site.
- Emails reach real people only after a domain is verified in Resend and `EMAIL_FROM` uses it; with `onboarding@resend.dev` Resend delivers only to the Resend account owner (seen 2026-10-03).
- Never run `prisma migrate reset` against a real database.
- Backups (#27): nightly `pg_dump` encrypted with `age` to an EU S3-compatible bucket (`.github/workflows/backup.yml`, `scripts/backup/dump.sh`, public key in `.github/backup-recipients.txt`), freshness check at 12:00 UTC, 30-day retention by the bucket's lifecycle rule. Setup, restore drill and recovery steps: `docs/backups.md`.
- Database changes (#30): `vercel-build` builds first, then migrates. Migrations are expand/contract (README, "Database changes"); the *Migrations* workflow (`scripts/check-migrations.mjs`, `src/lib/migration-check.mjs`) fails a PR with `DROP COLUMN`, `DROP TABLE`, `RENAME` or an edited/removed migration unless it has the label `breaking-migration`.
